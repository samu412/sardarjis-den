Our Story

Built For Food.
Not Just Business.

For Manmeet Singh, food has always been about more than what’s on the plate. It’s about the joy of cooking, the warmth of serving, and the happiness that comes from bringing people together over a great meal.

That passion became the foundation of Sardarji’s Den, a place built on his love for food and his belief that every meal should be an experience worth remembering.

Rooted in authentic Punjabi flavours and driven by a commitment to fresh preparation, Sardarji’s Den brings together distinctive Jhatka chicken and mutton, freshly prepared in-house paneer, sauces and a diverse menu spanning Indian, Continental and Oriental cuisines, alongside thoughtfully crafted drinks.

“Because for us, food was never just business. It was always personal.”

Manmeet Singh — Founder, Sardarji's Den
